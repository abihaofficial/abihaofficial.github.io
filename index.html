How a build runs

The same four stages, whether the work is a strategy, a coalition or a new programme. Each stage ends with something you can use on its own.

Listen

Interviews with leadership, staff, partners and funders. I find the tension everyone already knows about and nobody has written down.

Shape

A short, opinionated draft: what we will do, what we won't, who owns what, what it costs. Built for argument, then revised.

Line up

Partners, funders and team signed up to the same words. Proposals, briefings and the first workplan go out.

Run

I stay through launch, train the people who will carry it, and hand over a system rather than a slide deck.
