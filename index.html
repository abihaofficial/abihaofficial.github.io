about me! what's up

I studied management in Lahore, then global affairs in Beijing. Since then I have built a business in a second language, worked beside an Ambassador in Islamabad, joined an offshore wind non-profit in its first months in London, and led strategy at a Pakistani energy think tank.

The rooms change. The job doesn't. Someone has a big idea and no plan, or a plan and nobody moving. I like closing that gap, and I'm curious about how AI is changing who gets to tell the story.
