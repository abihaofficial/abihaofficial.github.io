What I offer

Programmes designed to run, not just to read

I take a new country or regional programme from concept to first delivery, shaping the model, the workplan, the partners and the training together with the team who will own it after I leave.

Strategy that boards and funders can act on

I write organisational strategies and funding proposals in plain language, built on interviews with leadership and programme teams, with clear choices, owners and risks that a board can decide on in one sitting.

Coalitions that agree before they meet

I bring governments, funders, think tanks and companies into shared positions, doing the drafting and member alignment ahead of a convening so the room can spend its time on decisions.

Communications that carry complex policy

I turn energy policy, development finance and technical analysis into speeches, briefs, press material and creator-led content that each audience can understand and repeat.
